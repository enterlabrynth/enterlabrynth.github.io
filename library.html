How to use EnterLabrynth

Every module teaches one topic in up to eight different forms. They cover the same material, so choose the ones that fit how you learn. This page lists all of them in rows you can swipe sideways. Inside a module, the same eight are the tabs across the top.

Flow chartsThe full lesson, one step at a time, with diagrams and animations you can tap.Start here when a topic is new.
NotesEverything you need to know on one page: short points, tables, common mix-ups and a checklist.Best for reviewing before an exam.
InfographicsEvery diagram from the flow charts, without the reading in between.Best if you remember pictures.
SlidesThe same lesson as a slide deck: one idea and one picture per slide.Best for a quick run-through, or for explaining it to someone else.
VideosShort narrated walkthroughs.Best if you like to watch and listen.
QuizMultiple choice. Each answer tells you straight away whether you were right, and why.Best for finding what you do not know yet.
Vocab cardsA flip card for every term. Say the definition, then tap to check.Best for vocabulary.
QuestionsThe guiding questions for the topic, each with a worked answer.Best for practising written answers from memory.
HOW TO TELL WHAT YOU CAN TAP
A green pill that says "Tap…"The picture below it is interactive. Tap its buttons, or the labels on the drawing.
Buttons that pulseThey have not been tried yet. They stop once you tap one.
Back and NextA picture with several stages. Step through them in order.
KEY TERMS · a row of small boxesTap a term to open its definition.
Check yourselfA question with the answer hidden. Decide first, then tap to open it.
A GOOD ORDER, IF YOU WANT ONEFlow charts or Videos to learn it. Notes to pull it together. Vocab cards and Questions to practise. Quiz to check.All eight cover the same material, so you never have to do all of them. Pick the ones that suit how you learn.
