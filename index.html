SSBPs

THE HOLDERS
Single-strand DNA-binding proteins, on the opened strands
POWERStabilize the single strands, holding them apart so they don't pair back up before they're copied. (Proteins, not enzymes.)
IF IT'S MISSINGThe opened strands snap back together, or tangle, before they can be copied.
